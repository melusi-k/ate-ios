# Shared recipes: rules and takedowns

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Anyone with a share link can see a shared recipe. When you share, you agree that the recipe:
- is yours, or you have the right to share it. Keep the original source link for recipes from websites or creators.
- contains no personal information about anyone (including you) that you don't want public.
- isn't illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent, or dangerous (for example unsafe food preparation presented as safe).
- contains no spam, malware or misleading links.

## Reporting a link or asking for a takedown
Email **melusi.l.k@gmail.com** with the subject **"Takedown"** and include:
1. the share link,
2. why it should come down (for example "it's my recipe, copied without credit", "it contains my personal details" or "it's harmful"),
3. for copyright claims: who you are, the original work, and a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised.

I'll look at it within **5 working days** and remove content that breaks these rules, or that its sharer asks me to remove. If I remove something you shared, I'll tell you if I can reach you, and you can reply if you think it was a mistake.

> ⚖️ **Lawyer review needed:** takedown process against the ECTA (South Africa, s77) and the EU Digital Services Act notice-and-action rules for hosting services.
