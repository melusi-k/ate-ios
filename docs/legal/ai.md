# AI disclosure

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

## What uses AI
- **Recipe cards:** Apple Intelligence (**Foundation Models**) turns a link, screenshot or prompt into a recipe card, and powers "Ask ate" rewrites and step timers.
- **Covers:** **Image Playground** draws a cover for recipes with no photo or web image. You can also tap **Generate cover** in a recipe's … menu.
- Links with structured recipe data are read directly, without AI.

## Where it runs
**Entirely on your iPhone.** There's no server-side AI, and your prompts, screenshots and recipes aren't sent to OpenAI, Google, me or anyone else. It needs iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence device (iPhone 15 Pro or newer). Without that, ate shows a clearly labelled sample recipe card, and covers fall back to a designed tile.

## ⚠️ AI can be wrong
AI-made recipes can have mistakes in **quantities, units, cooking times, temperatures, allergens and food-safety steps**. ate checks times and throws out implausible ones, but it can't catch everything.

- **Always read a recipe before you cook.**
- **Allergies and dietary needs:** never rely on ate to say a recipe is free of an allergen. Check every ingredient and its label yourself.
- **Food safety:** cook meat, poultry, fish and eggs to safe temperatures, whatever the recipe says.
- **Covers are illustrations**, not photos of the real dish.
- Health-based suggestions are not medical advice.

## Reporting a bad result
Edit the recipe in the app, or email **melusi.l.k@gmail.com** with what went wrong.
