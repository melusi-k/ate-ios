3. **Feedback.** You may send feedback, screenshots and test results. You let me use them to improve ate, with no payment owed. Don't include other people's personal information.
4. **Uploaded test results** are linked to your account and deleted with it. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
5. **Confidentiality.** Please don't publish unreleased features as finished; screenshots with "beta" context are fine.
6. **Ending.** You can stop testing at any time. I can end the beta or a tester's access at any time.
7. **Free.** The beta costs nothing and I promise no support times.
