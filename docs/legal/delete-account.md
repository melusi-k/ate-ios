# Delete your ate account

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open ate and go to **Settings › Account & sync**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Read the warning and confirm. You may need to sign in again first.

That's it. It happens straight away.

## Without the app
Email **melusi.l.k@gmail.com** from the address on your account, with the subject **"Delete my ate account"**. I'll confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually within a few days. I may ask you to confirm from that email address so nobody else can delete your account.

## What gets deleted
- Your account (email address, password hash and sign-in records).
- Your synced recipes and shopping list.
- Shares created by your account.
- Any beta test results you uploaded.

Database backups roll off within the hosting provider's normal cycle (up to about 7 days).

## What isn't deleted automatically
- **Older anonymous shared recipe links.** New shares require sign-in and are deleted with your account. Older anonymous links aren't tied to your account, so they may stay viewable; email me each link (for example `https://melusi-k.github.io/ate-legal/r/abc123`) and I'll remove it.
- **Data on your phone.** Deleting your account removes the local recipes, shopping list, food diary, nutrition goals and cooking history too. Sign out without choosing **remove local data** keeps the local copy; you can also remove it from the sign-out choice. Export first with **Settings › Export data** if you want a portable copy of recipes, shopping, diary entries and supported preferences/goals.
- **Apple Health** data was never stored by ate. To stop access, use iOS Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ate.
