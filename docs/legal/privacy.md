# ate Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

ate is a recipe app made by **Melusi Khumalo**, an individual developer based in South Africa ("I", "me"). This policy explains what ate stores, where it's stored, and the choices you have. Questions: **melusi.l.k@gmail.com**.

## The short version
- ate works without an account. With no account, your recipes, shopping list and settings stay on your device.
- If you create an account, ate stores your email address plus your synced recipes and shopping list so they show up on your other devices.
- Recipe AI runs **on your device** using Apple Intelligence. ate doesn't send your recipe content to any third-party AI service.
- No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

## What ate stores

**On your device:** your recipe library, shopping list, timer and app settings. This stays on the device unless you sign in to sync.

**If you create an account:**
- **Account data:** your email address, a securely hashed password (if you use one) and basic sign-in records (when the account was created and last signed in).
- **Synced content:** your recipes and shopping-list items, so they stay in sync across your devices.

**If you share a recipe:** ate publishes a read-only copy of that recipe (title, description, ingredients, steps and any image link) at a share link. **Anyone who has the link can view it.** Shared recipes aren't linked to your account.

**Recipe links and screenshots you import:** to read a recipe from a link, your device downloads that web page directly. Screenshots are read on your device with Apple's text recognition. Neither is uploaded to me.

## Where data is stored
Account and synced data are stored with **Supabase** (a hosted database and authentication provider) in its **EU (Ireland, eu-west-1)** region. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Supabase acts as a processor on my behalf. If your country is outside the EU, your data is transferred there and protected under Supabase's data processing terms.

## AI processing
ate uses **Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models) on your device** to turn links, screenshots and prompts into recipe cards. This processing happens on your iPhone. ate doesn't send your recipe content to OpenAI, Google or any other third-party AI provider. If on-device AI isn't available, ate shows a sample card instead.

## What ate doesn't do
- No advertising, and no ad or analytics SDKs.
- No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or renting of your data.
- No access to your contacts, location or photos beyond the single image you choose to import.

## Why I use your data (legal bases)
- **To provide the service you asked for** (sign-in, sync, sharing). This is contract performance.
- **To keep ate secure and working** (for example preventing abuse of share links). This is a legitimate interest.
- I don't use your data for marketing.

## How long data is kept
- **Account and synced data:** kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and synced recipes and shopping list are deleted from the database straight away. Backups held by the hosting provider roll off within their normal backup cycle (typically up to 7 days on Supabase's standard plans).
- **Shared recipes:** stay available at their link because they aren't tied to an account. Email me the link to have one removed.
- **Data on your device:** stays until you delete it, sign out and remove local data, or delete the app.

## Your rights (POPIA and GDPR)
Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and the GDPR if you're in the EU/EEA or UK, you can:
- access the personal information held about you,
- correct it,
- delete it (use **Settings › Account & sync › Delete account**, or email me),
- object to or restrict processing,
- receive your data in a portable format (use **Settings › Export data** for your recipes),
- complain to a regulator: in South Africa the **Information Regulator** (inforegulator.org.za), or your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email **melusi.l.k@gmail.com**. I'll reply within 30 days.

## Children
ate isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I'll delete it.

## Security
Passwords are hashed by the authentication provider. Database rules make sure each account can only read its own synced data, and connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, I'll update the date above. If the changes are significant, I'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.

## Apple Health (optional)
If you connect Apple Health, ate reads sleep, step count, active energy and workouts on your iPhone to suggest recipes and journal prompts. This is read-only. Health data is processed on your device, is never uploaded, synced, sold, or used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties. Ate journal entries are stored only on your device. You can revoke access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.
