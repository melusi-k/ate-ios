# Your data rights (POPIA and GDPR)

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Wherever you are, you get the same rights. South Africa's **POPIA** applies because ate is run from South Africa. The **GDPR** applies if you're in the EU, EEA or UK.

## Your rights and how to use them

| Right | Do it yourself | Or email me |
|---|---|---|
| **Access:** get a copy of what I hold | Settings › Export data (recipes, shopping, diary and supported local settings/goals) | ✓, for account and server data |
| **Correct** | Edit recipes in the app | ✓ |
| **Delete** | [Delete your account](delete-account/) | ✓, including shared links |
| **Portability** | Settings › Export data | ✓ |
| **Object or restrict** | Turn off sync or sign out | ✓ |
| **Withdraw consent** (Health, Journaling) | iOS Settings › Health / Privacy | — |
| **Not be subject to automated decisions** | ate makes no decisions with legal or similar effects about you | — |

## Making a request
1. Email **melusi.l.k@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data request"**, from your account's email address if you have one.
2. Say what you want (for example "a copy of my data" or "remove this share link").
3. I may ask you to confirm your identity, only enough to protect your data.
4. I'll reply within **30 days**. It's free. If a request is complex I'll tell you within that time and may take up to two more months (GDPR).

## Complaints
Please tell me first so I can fix it. You can also complain to a regulator at any time:
- **South Africa: Information Regulator.** [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za) · complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za · enquiries@inforegulator.org.za · JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg 2001.
- **EU/EEA:** your local data protection authority ([list](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en)). ate's data sits in Ireland, but I have no establishment in the EU, so you can complain in your own country; the Irish **Data Protection Commission** ([dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie)) is one option.
- **UK:** the ICO ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

## PAIA
South Africa's **Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA)** lets you request records held by a private body. As a one-person developer, I'll handle PAIA requests through the same email. A PAIA manual will be published here if one is required.
